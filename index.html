Deriv synthetic indices are generated randomly. EMA, RSI and ATR describe what already happened; they do not predict the next tick, and “wait for 2 against ticks, then enter” is a pattern the scanner tests rather than a proven edge. The track-record box shows how the signals did on the scanned history and in this session against the random baseline. If the rates sit near the baseline, the signals add nothing.
